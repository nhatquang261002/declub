# ADR 0045: Club challenges: the Owner sets a task, winners earn activity points

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-07 (amended: challenges run in epochs, and can be Holders-only; an entry earns only its prize: [ADR 0048](0048-entries-earn-through-their-challenge.md); a Holder vote, a raffle, first in and everyone who completes: [ADR 0049](0049-more-ways-to-pick-challenge-winners.md); trophies, an NFT or token prize and pinned results: [ADR 0050](0050-challenge-rewards-beyond-points.md)) · Web only: no contract change. Builds on [ADR 0038](0038-points-from-backing.md) (points) and [ADR 0044](0044-owner-reward-settings.md) (what Owners may set). The spec is [09 · Clubs](../09-clubs.md#challenges). Replaces the roadmap's "weekly prompt".

## Context

A Club's points mostly come from backing: members liking and replying to each other. Nothing lets an Owner point that activity at something, such as a meme contest, a best pitch or a naming vote, or reward the members who do it well. The roadmap sketched a *weekly* prompt on the Board. A fixed weekly cadence doesn't fit: Owners want to start one when something happens (a mint, a collab, a quiet week), not on a calendar. They can start one at any time, but it ends when an epoch does, so its points land in a known epoch.

The Owner choosing how many points a winner gets is a power to tune the point system toward themselves or friends, which ADR 0044 deliberately kept from Owners. So whatever the Owner sets has to stay inside bounds the protocol fixes, and has to be checkable.

## Decision

- **A challenge is started by the Owner (or an app admin) at any time** and runs from that moment **to the end of an epoch**: this one (`epochs: 1`) or one of the next three (up to 4), with at least an hour left. It has a title (60 characters), a brief (280), who judges, the points per place, and a `holdersOnly` flag. At most 3 are open at once, so the Board isn't buried. The Owner can cancel it until it is settled. `POST /api/clubs/[token]/challenges`; logged (`create_challenge`, `cancel_challenge`, `settle_challenge`).
- **Points are the Owner's to set, within protocol bounds** (`CHALLENGE_RULES`, `CHALLENGE_CAP_PER_EPOCH`): 1 to 3 paid places, each a whole number of points from 1 to 20, a lower place never more than a higher one. Whatever a member wins, at most **20 challenge points count for them in one epoch**: the wins are all kept and published, the epoch counts the first 20. Challenge points are ordinary activity points (`activityPoints`'s `challenge`): they raise the bonus (up to +25%) and move a Holder up the ramp. They never pay anyone directly, and a member without NFTs only benefits in a Club that gives them a slice.
- **Holders only (optional).** With `holdersOnly` set, only members with the Holder badge can enter (refused with `holders_only`); anyone in the Club can still like. The badge is the Club's own (the NFT, or the memecoin where the Owner counts it), as for "holders only can post".
- **Members answer with a Board post.** A member enters one of their own top-level posts made while the challenge is open (`POST …/challenges/[id] { action: "enter", post }`); the app's form signs and posts it in one step. **The entry is an X post** (`xEntryProblem`): the Board post must share the member's own X post (from the X account linked to their profile, the same `own` test as X shares, ADR 0024), posted on X after the challenge started and before it ends (`not_x`, `x_not_own`, `x_too_old`). A challenge then puts the Club on X, and an old post can't be recycled. Entries keep the X post's card (`PublicEntry.x`); whether it also counts as an X share is the X-share rules' business, as for any shared post. One entry each (a new one replaces the old), a post is an entry in one challenge, the Owner and app admins can't enter, nor can members banned from the Club or from rewards. Using the Board post means likes, replies, signatures, reports and moderation all work as they do already, and a hidden or deleted entry drops out.
- **Two ways to pick winners**, chosen when the challenge starts:
  - **Likes** (`judging: "likes"`): entries are ranked by distinct likes from other members who are still in the Club and not banned from rewards, the earlier entry first on a tie, at least one like to win, a member wins once. The likes of an entry are **locked from the end** (`votingClosed`: a like or a taken-back like is refused with `voting_closed`), so the tally can't move between the end and the settling.
  - **The Owner** (`judging: "owner"`): after it ends the Owner has 14 days to name the winners, best first (`action: "pick"`). Likes aren't locked; the Owner may weigh them or not. If they don't pick in time the challenge ends with no winners (`expired`).
- **Settling is lazy and idempotent.** `settleDue` settles every challenge that has ended, when challenges are read, when the live standings are worked out, and in the epoch close job before inputs are frozen. A lock per challenge and a win keyed by challenge id mean a repeat never pays twice.
- **Which epoch the points count in.** A likes-judged challenge ends with an epoch and counts in it, if that epoch hasn't been closed yet, else in the running one; an Owner-judged one counts in the epoch of the pick. Wins are kept per epoch (`declub:challenge:{chain}:{club}:awards:{n}`), read when the epoch's inputs are frozen (`ClosingHolder.challenge`), so a frozen epoch never changes. A member banned from rewards wins nothing.
- **A card and a page of their own.** A challenge isn't a one-line system message. On the Board and the timeline it is a **card** (a striped trophy band with its state, the title and brief, the prizes as gold, silver and bronze tiles that name the winners once settled, a clock bar with the time left and the entry count, the leading entries ranked, and a button into it), set among the posts by time when it starts ("New challenge") and when it is won ("Challenge results"). On the Club's Board a challenge that is still running (open, counting likes, or waiting on the Owner's pick) is **pinned on top** of the posts (whatever the order or filter), and so it is on the timeline, with its Club named (`GET /api/timeline/challenges?scope=joined|all`, `timelineChallenges`), so its start isn't repeated in the feed (`withoutPinned`; the timeline pins only the first three and leaves every start out, per the 2026-10-09 amendment of [ADR 0041](0041-timeline.md)); several pinned at once leave their entries out. Once it is settled or cancelled it falls back into the feed at the time it started, with its result. The same card is on the Club's Challenges tab. Each challenge has its own page, `/clubs/[address]/challenges/[id]`, with the brief, the box where members write their entry, every entry ranked with its likes, and the Owner's place picker (a 1st/2nd/3rd toggle on each entry) and cancel (`GET /api/clubs/[token]/challenges/[id]`). The feed reads the start and the result from the stored challenges (`SystemEvent` kinds `challenge` and `winner`, with the challenge id); `weave` makes each its own item, splitting any run of system messages. Nothing new is kept.
- **Public.** The points ledger of each epoch carries `challengePoints` per member and `challenges: { cap, wins[] }` with every win (challenge id, title, wallet, place, points), so anyone can see where a member's points came from and recompute them. The live standings and the Points meter show them.

## Consequences

- An Owner can still favour friends: in an owner-judged challenge they pick, and in a likes-judged one friends' likes count. The bounds contain it: at most 20 points per member per epoch, 3 places, the Owner can't enter, every challenge and pick is in the Club's log and published with the epoch, and members the Owner shouldn't reward can be banned only by someone who outranks them. It's the same trust as the Owner's other settings (ADR 0044), with a smaller blast radius than letting them set rules.
- Like rings can win a likes-judged challenge. Likes come from members who joined the Club, a member can't like their own entry, likes lock at the end, and the prize is bounded; a Club that sees farming can switch to Owner judging or ban the accounts from rewards. No new anti-sybil work here: backing already has voices and pair caps, but a challenge's likes don't use them.
- Challenge points reach only the epoch's points. Holding still sets the size of a share (NFTs held, averaged); a member with 20 challenge points and no activity otherwise is paid a whole ramp share of their NFTs and a 14% bonus (20/35 of the 25% cap), not more.
- Moderators may enter. So staff can't clear the field, a Moderator who has entered a challenge that is still running or being judged can't hide, delete or ban a member who is in the same one (`rivals`, refused with `challenge_conflict`); the Owner, an app admin or another Moderator has to. Once it is settled the restriction ends.
- Entering costs a Board post, so it also counts as an active day and is rate limited like any post.
- Nothing about challenges is on chain, and the Board, momentum and the deal contracts are unchanged.
