# ADR 0050: Challenge rewards beyond points: trophies, an NFT or token prize the Owner sends, pinned results

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-08 · Web only: no contract change. Amends [ADR 0045](0045-club-challenges.md) (challenges) and [ADR 0049](0049-more-ways-to-pick-challenge-winners.md) (its "rewards beside the points"); [ADR 0048](0048-entries-earn-through-their-challenge.md) (what an entry earns) and [ADR 0044](0044-owner-reward-settings.md) (Owners can't raise protocol caps) hold. The spec is [09 · Clubs](../09-clubs.md#challenges).

## Context

A challenge's prize was points only: activity points that raise the bonus and the ramp. That suits a Club whose members already hold, but many Owners want to give something a winner can keep or show: a badge of the win, an NFT from the collection or some tokens, and a moment in the spotlight on the Board.

Points are counted by the protocol and capped per member per epoch, so an Owner can't inflate the ramp. Anything else has to stay out of that count. An asset prize also can't be held by the app: DeClub has no escrow for it and shouldn't promise one. What the app can do is check, on chain, that the Owner sent what they promised, and say so plainly when they didn't.

## Decision

- **A `rewards` beside the points**, optional on the input, the stored and the public challenge: `{ asset?, pinWinner? }`. `checkChallenge` checks it (`checkRewards`), and challenges kept before read and settle unchanged. No epoch, ledger or standings code reads it: `prizes` stays the points, and the per-member cap (`CHALLENGE_CAP_PER_EPOCH`) is untouched.
- **A trophy for every winner, always.** When a challenge settles (`finish`), each winner gets a trophy, in every mode and for a 0-point win too: `{ club, challenge, title, place, at, wallet }`, `place` 1 to 3 for a ranked win and `null` for a flat one ("Winner"). Kept per user across Clubs at `declub:challenge:{chain}:trophies:{userId}` (newest first, at most 200), replacing one for the same challenge, so a repeat settle gives one trophy. A cancelled challenge gives none. A wallet's profile (`GET /api/wallets/[address]/profile`, `trophies`) shows its trophies as medals (gold, silver, bronze, gold "Winner"), each linking to its challenge; a Club's Members list shows a trophy count on the member's row, the titles in its tooltip. Both show only the wins published under that wallet, so neither ties together wallets their owner kept apart.
- **An NFT or token prize (optional): the Owner sends it, the app checks it.**
  - **What can be offered:** one asset on the verified list (status `verified`; a blocked or unlisted contract is refused), an ERC-721 (a whole number of NFTs, 1 to `maxPrizeNfts`, 5) or an ERC-20 (an amount in token units, up to `maxPrizeTokens`, 1,000,000,000). A ranked challenge gives an amount per paid place, never more for a lower place (a lower place may get none); a flat one gives one amount, what each winner gets. An ERC-20 amount is converted with the decimals its contract reports: the server reads them itself when the challenge starts (`PrizeChain.decimals`); a token whose decimals can't be read can't be a prize. Kept as `rewards.asset: { token, kind, name, symbol, decimals?, amounts }`, amounts in base units (or NFTs) as strings.
  - **Owed.** When it settles, each winner with an amount has `prize` on their `Winner`; the server keeps who they are (`prizeUsers`, never public) to read their linked wallets.
  - **Sent.** The Owner (or an app admin) sends it from their own wallet, then reports it: `POST …/challenges/[id] { action: "prize_sent", post, tx }`. The server reads the receipt (`PrizeChain.receipt`, viem in production, a fake in tests) and accepts it only if it succeeded, its block is at or after the settlement, and it holds `Transfer` logs of the asset's own contract to any of the winner's linked wallets (or the wallet the win was published under) adding up to at least the amount (ERC-20: the summed value; ERC-721: distinct token ids). Refusals: `tx_not_found`, `tx_failed`, `tx_too_early`, `wrong_asset`, `wrong_recipient`, `too_little`, `chain_unavailable`. A transaction pays one prize (`declub:challenge:{chain}:prize-tx:{hash}`, `tx_used`). The winner's `sent` is `{ tx, at }`; logged (`prize_sent`).
  - **Overdue.** A prize not sent 14 days after the settle (`prizeDueMs`) is overdue: the card and the challenge's page say "Prize not sent". While any prize of the Club is overdue, a new challenge with an asset prize is refused (`prize_overdue`); a points-only one isn't.
  - **Never promised by the app.** The copy says the Owner sends it and the app checks the transfer; DeClub never holds a prize and can't send it for them.
  - **Notifications:** the winner gets `challenge-prize-sent` once theirs is checked; the Owner and admins get an actionable `challenge-prize-owed` while any is owed, news again once overdue (key suffix `:overdue`), whatever its age.
- **Pin the winners (optional `pinWinner`).** For 3 days after it settles (`pinResultsMs`), a challenge with winners stays pinned on top of the Board as "Challenge results", the winning entry (1st place, or a flat prize's winners) drawn out in full. It reuses the pinned-challenge mechanism (`isPinned`, `PublicChallenge.pinUntil`); while pinned, neither its start nor its result is repeated in the feed (`withoutPinned`'s `results`). The Board's pinned posts and the timeline are unchanged.
- **The Owner's form** has a fifth step, **Rewards**, after the prize: a card saying every winner gets a trophy, **Also give an NFT or token** (a verified-asset picker with its trust chip, and an amount per place or for each winner, with the three steps: winners are picked, you send it, you paste the transaction), and **Pin the winners on the Board for 3 days**. The live preview shows the asset under the points on the prize tiles, and the form runs the same `checkChallenge` with the decimals it read.

## Consequences

- A winner can keep and show a win, and a Club can offer something real, without the app taking custody or the protocol's points moving.
- The app can't make an Owner pay. What it does is make not paying visible ("Prize not sent" on the card and the page, in the Owner's bell) and stop the same Owner from offering another asset prize in that Club until they settle up. Members decide how much an Owner's promise is worth.
- One transaction pays one prize, so an Owner paying several winners sends several transactions (or batches and reports one per winner). A transfer from any wallet counts: it doesn't matter who sent it, only that the winner received it after the settle.
- An Owner can send to the wrong wallet of a winner: the server accepts any wallet the winner has linked. A winner who unlinked a wallet can still be paid at the wallet their win was published under.
- Trophies are kept per user and filtered by wallet when shown, so a member who changes the wallet the Members list shows keeps their trophies, but they show under the wallet that won.
- Old challenges have no `rewards` and read as before; trophies start with the challenges settled from now on. Nothing is migrated.
