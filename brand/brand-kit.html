The De(al) Club brand kit: a pinboard of trading stickers. Cream paper, thick ink outlines, a strip of red tape, and warm gold for the one thing to press. Written rules are in docs/brand-kit.md.

Logo

A “D” with a dot set into its bowl, on a tile with its own ink outline. In the words, “(al)” is smaller and dimmed so “Deal” hides inside “De(al) Club”.

Size and space

  • Clear space: a quarter of the tile's height on every side.
  • Minimum size: mark 16 px; full logo 120 px wide. Below that, use the mark.
16 px 32 px 120 px

Don't

  • Recolour the mark or the wordmark
  • Stretch, rotate or add shadows and glows
  • Drop the dot from the D
  • Set the words in Geist or another face
  • Write “(al)” at full strength or full size
  • Put the tile where its outline disappears

Colour

Ink and cream carry the page. Gold is for the single thing to press. Red is for emphasis. Blue and mint are signals. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

Shape and surface

A 2 px ink outline on everything. Cream panels on the canvas. Tape on one panel per page. No shadows: depth comes from outline, tilt and overlap.

Voice

Short, direct, warm. Playful look, literal words about money.

We say

  • “Each completed swap pays a small fee.”
  • “Assets move only in transactions or signatures you approve.”
  • “Your community’s market, owned by its members.”
  • Swap and deal; Club, Holder, Owner, Board.

We don't say

  • Anything that promises returns or prices.
  • “On Deal”, “Declub”, “DECLUB”, “De(al)Club”.
  • Rounded or vague fee and reward numbers.
  • Slang that dates it (proposed).
